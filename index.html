01 / OUR MISSION

Understanding systems.
Anticipating impact.

At ConSEQuence Lab, we study what advanced computing systems can do, how they work, where they fail, and what their development means for the world around us.

Our work connects artificial intelligence, quantum computing, computational discovery, and security. We bring engineering and rigorous evaluation together to advance systems that are capable, interpretable, and trustworthy.
